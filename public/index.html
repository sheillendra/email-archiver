Gmail email archiving app for Windows

EmailArchiver

EmailArchiver helps users scan old Gmail messages, save verified copies as local email archive files on their Windows computer, and read those archived emails offline.

The app connects to Gmail with Google OAuth, downloads selected older messages, verifies the local archive, and only moves Gmail messages to Trash when the user explicitly enables that option.

App purpose

What EmailArchiver does

EmailArchiver is designed for people who need to reduce old Gmail storage, keep a searchable local copy of important email records, and access archived email files without relying on a mail server.

01

Scans older Gmail messages

Users choose an email age range, scan Gmail metadata, and review the message list before archiving.

02

Saves local archives

Messages are saved on the user's computer as organized email archive files per Gmail account.

03

Reads archived email offline

Users can search subjects, senders, recipients, and attachments from the local archive without reconnecting Gmail.

04

Optionally cleans Gmail

After successful local verification, users may choose to move archived Gmail messages to Trash.

Keamanan dan privasi

Gmail baru disentuh setelah arsip lokal terbukti aman

Email ditulis ke file sementara, dihitung hash SHA-256, lalu diverifikasi setelah menjadi arsip final. Opsi pembersihan memakai Trash, bukan penghapusan permanen.

OAuth resmi GooglePassword Gmail tidak pernah diminta atau disimpan.
Token terenkripsi DPAPICredential aplikasi dilindungi per pengguna Windows.
Protected ArchiveMode arsip terenkripsi AES-256-GCM untuk email sensitif.
HTML dibatasiScript, iframe, popup, dan resource jarak jauh diblokir.

Lisensi

Trial satu bulan, aktivasi offline, tetap bisa membaca arsip

Lisensi penuh terikat Machine ID dan diverifikasi secara lokal. Jika lisensi tidak aktif, aplikasi tetap membuka mode read-only agar pengguna tidak kehilangan akses baca terhadap arsip yang sudah dibuat.
